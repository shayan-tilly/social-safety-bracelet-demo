The community we protect

Built first for college nightlife.

Our initial market is young adults navigating parties, campus events, concerts, and unfamiliar nightlife—especially people who may become intoxicated, separated from friends, or unable to ask for help.

Primary protected user

College-age friends who want freedom without losing their safety net.

The experience strengthens the care that already exists inside trusted groups. It does not ask strangers to monitor users or assume one universal idea of risk.

Design boundaries

Agency comes before automation.

The product is designed to support human judgment, not replace it. Privacy and honest technical claims are part of the prototype—not future cleanup work.

◌

Quiet by default

Haptic and private signals avoid escalating a dangerous situation or exposing the wearer’s request for help.

◎

Consent-led sharing

Users choose their trusted circle, session duration, and escalation path. Location is not permanently visible.

✦

Multisignal, not diagnosis

Breathing, oxygen trend, heart rate, motion, and responsiveness can suggest possible distress, but cannot identify an overdose or substance.

⚠
Prototype limitation

This experience uses simulated physiological signals. Wrist readings can be affected by movement, device fit, circulation, temperature, skin characteristics, and other factors. Joy & Whimsy does not diagnose intoxication, overdose, drink spiking, assault, consent, or a specific medical emergency, and it is not a replacement for emergency services.
