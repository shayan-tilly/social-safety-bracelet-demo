Phone unavailable scenario

The Guardian Charm handles the alert.

The central charm contains the proposed safety hardware. It uses the phone when available to conserve battery, but the intended standalone version has its own connection for essential alerts.

Prototype boundary: This page visualizes the intended standalone architecture. The current web prototype does not contain a physical cellular modem, GPS radio, medical sensors, or an activated eSIM.
